Full-Stack Commerce Capabilities

Everything You Need to Scale Wholesale Operations

From catalog management to warehouse dispatch and invoice settlement, OrderHub streamlines every touchpoint.

🛡️

Role-Based Access Control (RBAC)

Granular cryptographic JWT session protection. Differentiate portals for wholesale buyers, regional sales reps, and warehouse operations leads with tailored views.

📦

Real-Time Inventory & Stock Clamping

Live warehouse stock levels. Intelligent quantity clamping prevents over-ordering, low-stock threshold badges notify buyers, and out-of-stock items lock automatically.

🧾

Automated Net-14/30 Invoicing

Instant generation of authorized commercial tax invoices upon order confirmation. Line items, 10% tax breakdown, payment deadlines, and printable PDF documents.

🔄

Order Lifecycle Fulfillment

Transition orders smoothly from Pending Review ➔ Confirmed ➔ Processing ➔ Shipped with carrier tracking ➔ Delivered, keeping stakeholders informed in real-time.

📝

Immutable Compliance Audit Trail

Every user sign-in, price adjustment, inventory override, and status update is immutably timestamped and recorded for regulatory compliance and dispute resolution.

🔌

High-Throughput REST API

Built for ERP and warehouse management integrations (WMS). Fast, standardized JSON endpoints for products, orders, invoices, and automated webhooks.
